# Riverside Community Hub - Staff Handover Guide

This guide is for the Riverside Community Hub team. It explains how to use the
website to manage members, bookings, and donations. You do not need any
technical knowledge to follow it.

## Who can do what

The site has four kinds of users:

- **Visitors** are members of the public who have not logged in. They can look
  at your facilities, see the donation drive, and make a donation.
- **Members** are people who have signed up. They can book rooms and equipment
  and manage their own bookings.
- **Staff** (that is most of your team) can approve or reject bookings, manage
  the list of rooms and equipment, and see the member list.
- **Admin** (usually a manager) can do everything staff can, and also see the
  donation reports and set up donation campaigns.

## Logging in

1. Go to the website and click **Log in** in the top right corner.
2. Enter the email and password for your staff or admin account.
3. Once you log in, you will see extra menu items at the top, including
   **Admin**, which is your control panel.

If you forget your password, ask your admin to help reset it.

## Approving or rejecting bookings

When a member requests to book a room or piece of equipment, it does not happen
automatically. It waits for a staff member to approve it. This lets you keep
control of your spaces.

1. Log in and click **Admin** at the top.
2. Look at the **Pending bookings** section. Each row shows the resource, the
   date, and the time requested.
3. Click **Approve** to confirm the booking, or **Reject** to decline it.
4. The member is automatically told of your decision inside their account.

The system will not let two bookings overlap for the same room. If a time is
already taken, a new request for that same time is blocked automatically, so
you do not have to worry about double-bookings.

## Seeing your members

1. In the **Admin** panel, find the **Members** section.
2. You will see a list of everyone who has signed up, with their membership
   type and when they joined.
3. Use the **search box** to find a member by name.

## Managing rooms and equipment

Your bookable spaces and items are called "resources". To keep the list
accurate:

1. In the **Admin** panel, find the resources section.
2. You can add a new room or piece of equipment, edit an existing one, or remove
   one that is no longer available.

Members will immediately see the updated list when they go to book.

## Donation reports (admin only)

If you are an admin, you can see how much has been donated.

1. Log in as an admin and click **Admin**.
2. Find the **Donation report** section. It shows the total raised, the amount
   this month, and a list of individual donations.
3. To keep a copy or share it with your board or funders, click **Export CSV**.
   This downloads a spreadsheet file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

## Donation campaigns (admin only)

A campaign is a fundraising drive with a goal, like "R50,000 for winter
parcels". The public sees a progress bar that fills up as donations come in.

1. As an admin, you can create a new campaign with a title and a goal amount.
2. You can turn a campaign on or off, so only the drives you want to show are
   visible to the public.

## Membership renewals

Memberships have an expiry date. When a member is within 30 days of their
membership ending, they see a gentle reminder on their dashboard that says their
membership is expiring soon.

To renew a member, an admin updates that member's expiry date to a new future
date. There is no online payment in this version, so renewals are handled by
your team the way you normally collect fees.

## A few things to know

- **Donations do not take card payments in this version.** The site records the
  amount and who gave it, so you have a clear record, but the actual money is
  collected the way you already do it.
- **Members can cancel their own bookings.** If they do, that time slot becomes
  free again for someone else.
- **Nothing is ever fully deleted by accident.** A cancelled or rejected booking
  is kept as a record, it just no longer holds the time slot.

## Getting help

If something does not look right, note what you were doing and what you saw, and
pass it to whoever manages the website for you. Clear notes make problems much
faster to fix.
